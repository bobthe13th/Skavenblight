# Palette's Journal

## 2026-07-31 - Minecraft Screen Gauge Tooltips
**Learning:** Custom block screens in Minecraft benefit immensely from interactive tooltips over multi-line custom status bars or energy gauges, using `isMouseAboveArea` coordinates combined with localized strings. This pattern makes implicit/hidden progression and required criteria explicit, significantly reducing guesswork for players.
**Action:** When designing a new Minecraft container GUI with custom resource bars or progress indicators, always map screen regions to multi-line, styled, localized tooltips to provide interactive feedback on hidden stats, recipe progress, and custom energy levels.
