# Palette's Journal — Critical UX/A11y Learnings

## 2024-11-20 - [Dynamic Meter Tooltips & Accessible Values]
**Learning:** Custom visual bar gauges (such as the Wind level meter and research progress indicators) lack textual clarity by default. Hover-only or visual-only meters fail to communicate precise values, requirements, or beneficial modifiers (such as the continuous speed multiplier) to the player, leading to confusion about why an action is slow or blocked.
**Action:** Always complement custom visual indicators with rich, multi-line, color-coded tooltips that explicitly list current vs. required numbers and translate complex mathematical multipliers into clear percentages (e.g., "+X% research speed").
