## 2026-07-31 - Tooltips for Custom Screen Gauges and Progress Bars
**Learning:** Custom block screens (like the Warp Flux Furnace and the Research Table) contain custom meters, progress indicators, and status gauges. While visually descriptive, these custom drawing zones do not inherently support screen reader descriptions or display exact numerical/formulaic variables (like active recipes, precise progress, or complex wind speed multipliers) by default. Implementing `isMouseAboveArea` checks paired with multi-line, localized, styled tooltips provides a clean, native-feeling, and fully accessible interface.
**Action:** Always map custom screen rendering bounds (`METER_X`, `PROGRESS_X`, etc.) to interactive mouse hover areas. Render dynamic, colored, multi-line tooltips using `guiGraphics.renderComponentTooltip` to show raw values, state contexts (e.g., active spell names, idle states), and continuous multipliers.
